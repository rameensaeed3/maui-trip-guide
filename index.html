Day 3 · June 2

Road to Hana

Full day · Depart 7 AM · Return ~7 PM
Prep the night before
Open the Shaka Guide app. Fill gas tank in Kahului on the way out. Pack: water, snacks, layers, closed-toe shoes. Bring cash for roadside stands and banana bread.
7:00 AM
Depart condo → Paia Town
35 min to Paia, the last town before the Hana Highway. Fill gas. Breakfast at Paia Fish Market or a local café.
Paia Fish Market — Laurie's pick
8:15 AM
Stop 1 — Rainbow Eucalyptus Trees
Short flat walk to massive trees with multicolored peeling bark. One of the most photographed spots on the road.
Free · Flat · 10 min
8:45 AM

500+ tropical plants, flat paths, ocean views. Open 8am–4pm. Best stop for non-hikers on the road.
~$20/personFlat · Accessible · Gorgeous

Day 4 · June 3

Haleakalā + Upcountry Maui

No sunrise reservation needed · Depart 9 AM
Pack warm layers — both of you
The summit is 10,023 feet and 30–40°F in June with strong winds. Pack real jackets, long pants, and a hat. No sunrise reservation needed after 7 AM — just pay $30/car at the gate and drive up anytime.
9:00 AM
Depart → Haleakalā Summit
~45 min drive. Tropical coast to upcountry farms to above-cloud volcanic moonscape.
$30/car park entranceLaurie's pick · 376 locals recommend
10:00 AM
Summit Visitor Center + Crater Views
Free exhibits on the volcano's geology and rare silversword plants. Walk to the Summit Overlook and Kalahaku Overlook — standing at 10,023 feet above the clouds is genuinely otherworldly. Allow 2 hours.
12:30 PM
Makawao Town — Upcountry Maui
Charming paniolo (cowboy) town — art galleries, boutique shops, cool and lush. Quick stop at Sacred Garden of Maliko, a serene botanical sanctuary just outside town.

Legendary cream puffs, malasadas, and stick donuts. Sells out — arrive by 1pm.
Sells out fast — arrive early
1:30 PM

3:00 PM

Goat dairy farm with tours and fresh cheese tastings — a fun, unique Maui experience.
